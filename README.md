# Mangwanani Art Gallery

A website for **Mangwanani Art Gallery**, a Zimbabwean art business specialising in authentic Shona stone sculptures, abstracts, handcrafts, and other African handmade products sold internationally.

## Pages
- **Home** — Image carousel and gallery preview
- **Portfolio** — Full sculpture gallery with lightbox viewer
- **About** — History of Shona art and the gallery's story
- **Contact** — Enquiry form and social links

## Tech Stack
- HTML5 / CSS3
- Bootstrap 3
- jQuery
- Font Awesome 4
- Lightbox2 (gallery viewer)
